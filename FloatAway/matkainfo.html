Travel Info

Here you will find important information about Float Away and its destinations!

What does the price include?

A part of the price includes traveling to your selected destination, your own cabin, a delicious breakfast!

Who is the travel for?

Float Away is for everyone! Whether you are a family wanting to take a break, a lone adventurer, or an alien from space!

When should you travel?

Anytime! Summer offers beautiful scenery without the cold, but the Winter offers the true nordic experience!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I travel with kids!

Yes! Absolutely, Float Away offers a great time for kids.

Do I need to pay beforehand?

Yes, you should purchase a ticket beforehand so you get your cabin.

What should I take with me?

Food, clothes, and most importantly a camera for the beautiful scenery! Money is also useful for souvenirs.
